ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Read the news. Follow the possible causes.

Global headlines come from a curated set of established international and regional newsrooms. Official policy sources provide laws, regulatory decisions, and economic announcements for context. Availability is shown below.

How top headlines rank

Fresh stories, high placement in publisher feeds, major political and economic topics, and similar coverage across different outlet groups affect the order. A diversity adjustment keeps one newsroom from filling the entire feed. RSS order is a proxy for prominence; this is not a measured worldwide popularity chart or a numerical trust score. Choose Latest first for chronological order.

What the suggestions mean

The context engine compares subjects, timing, entities, and possible mechanisms. It also looks for explicit attributed explanations. These rankings measure relevance, not the probability that one event caused another. Related coverage of a headline does not establish a causal link. No generative AI is used.

What you can read here

Publisher RSS excerpts are shown with attribution inside this page. They are labeled as excerpts, not full articles. Permitted official source text is loaded in full when available. Use Original publication for the publisher’s complete article; some require a subscription.

Coverage and limits

This prototype covers stories from around the world through English-language feeds. It does not cover every country, language, or publisher. Context searches use current news feeds plus searchable official US and European policy records; there is no complete historical news archive yet. Region labels are inferred from headline text and feed coverage. The globe filters by country and city mentions in the currently loaded reports. A country with no matching stories has no coverage in this feed; it does not mean nothing happened there.
